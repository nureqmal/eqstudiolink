Frequently Asked Questions

How does online booking actually work?

You get a personal link (eqstudio.link/book.html?slug=...). Customers open it, pick a date and open slot, fill in their details, and submit.

When is the deposit requested, and how do customers pay?

Right after a booking is confirmed, the system emails a deposit request automatically.

Can I ask customers extra questions during booking?

Yes. Add "Additional Questions" in Booking Settings.

Can I set my available hours and buffer time?

You can. Set your operating days and hours in Booking Settings.

Can customers reschedule or cancel on their own?

Every booking confirmation email comes with a "Reschedule / Cancel" button built right in.

Do I get proper invoices?

You do. Every reminder email comes with a PDF invoice attached.

Can customers check their own payment status?

Every email includes a personal link where customers can check whether they've paid.

Can I offer more than one type of service with different pricing?

Add "Service Types" (Event Types) in Booking Settings.

Can I run free bookings, like a RM0 consultation?

Type 0 into the Default Deposit field, or set it per service type.

Can I run more than one business on a single account?

That's what the Pro plan is for.

Is there reporting or analytics?

The dashboard's Reports page shows income trends and risk scoring.

Can customers use a different language?

Customers choose their own language when booking.

How does recurring payment work for repeat customers?

Mark "Recurring Payment" when adding a customer and pick an interval.

Is my customer data safe?

Every account can only see its own customer data. (Rujuk Dasar Privasi kami.)

Do I need a credit card for the 14-day trial?

No. Sign up with just an email and try it out before committing to anything.

Can I cancel my subscription anytime?

Yes, there's no lock-in contract.

How fast can I actually get set up?

A guided setup walks you through picking your business type, naming your first service, setting your hours, and your deposit.

Can I see a customer's full history in one place?

Yes. Open any customer's profile to see everything they've booked, what's paid vs pending, and their upcoming appointments.

Can I suggest features, and see what's coming?

Post a suggestion in the forum, other owners can vote on it.
